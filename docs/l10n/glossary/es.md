- **Register and address**: Write in neutral pan-Hispanic Spanish using **tú** (never *usted* or *vos*, and avoid regionalisms like *vosotros* or *ordenador*). Keep sentences short and direct—one idea per sentence—favoring everyday verbs (*comprobar*, *arreglar*, *añadir*, *escoger*) just like the existing pages.
- **Quotes and punctuation**: Use Spanish angular quotes `« »` in prose and inside double-quoted JSX attributes (`code="…"`), always include opening `¿` and `¡`, use the single ellipsis character `…`, and keep lowercase after a colon unless it introduces a quote or a full standalone sentence.
- **Capitalization**: Use sentence case for all headings, callout titles, buttons, and table headers (`Qué cambia cuando lo instalas`, `Deberías ver`, `Preguntas frecuentes`); never copy English Title Case.
- **Gender consistency and author neutrality**: Keep grammatical gender steady across pages (*la IA* feminine; *pstack* masculine; *la skill / las skills* feminine; *el playbook*, *el PR*, *el prompt*, *el commit*, *el diff*, *el test* masculine). Never apply gendered pronouns or nouns (*él*, *ella*, *el creador*) to **poteto**—use the name `poteto` and take advantage of Spanish omitted subjects (`poteto creó…`, `mantiene…`).
- **Clean developer Spanish**: Leave established English dev nouns bare without italics (*PR*, *commit*, *diff*, *prompt*, *tokens*, *tests*), but avoid Spanglish verbs (*commitear*, *mergear*, *pushear*); write natural Spanish verb phrases (*hacer commit*, *fusionar*, *subir*).
